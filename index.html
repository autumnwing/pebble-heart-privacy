Privacy Policy for Pebble & Heart

Last updated: 17 November 2026

The short version. Pebble & Heart is a puzzle game. There are no accounts and no sign-in, and we collect nothing about you ourselves: your scores and settings stay on your phone. Two things do reach the internet: the optional Game Center leaderboards, which are run by Apple, and the adverts, which are run by Google. Both are explained in full below.

Who this is from

Pebble & Heart is made by Leeknboat. If you have a question about privacy, write to leeknboat@proton.me.

What is stored on your phone

All of the following stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game:

  • Your best scores in Classic and Speed mode, your best combo, and simple totals (games played, pebbles and heart groups cleared)
  • Your settings: sound, music, haptics, reduce motion, whether to show How to play, and which scene you picked
  • Scores waiting to be sent to Game Center, if you were offline when you set them

We never receive any of it.

Game Center leaderboards

If you are signed in to Game Center on your device, the game sends your high scores to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on the leaderboards. Apple handles that data under Apple's Privacy Policy; we only see the public leaderboard, like everyone else. If you are not signed in to Game Center, nothing is sent and the game works the same way.

Advertising

The game is free and paid for by adverts from Google AdMob: a small banner at the top of the play screen and on the menu, an occasional full-screen advert between games, and an optional video you can choose to watch to continue a game after it ends. No advert interrupts a game in progress. Advertising is the only part of the game that sends data to anyone other than Apple, and Google, not us, receives the following:

What Google receivesWhy
Your IP addressChoosing adverts, measurement and fraud prevention. An IP address indicates roughly which region you are in.
Advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking, and similar device identifiersChoosing adverts and measurement.
Interactions with the advertMeasuring whether an advert was seen, tapped or watched to the end.
Diagnostic informationDetecting faults and fraudulent activity.

Google's own explanation of how it handles this data is at business.safety.google/privacy.

Your choices about advertising

  • Tracking. iOS asks whether the game may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your advertising identifier is not shared and you still get adverts, just less personalised ones. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent. Where local law requires it, including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states, the game shows Google's consent form before any advert is requested. You can change your answer later in the game's settings under Ad privacy choices.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no email address collected
  • No analytics or usage tracking of our own
  • Nothing sold or shared with data brokers
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location

Children

Pebble & Heart is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it, and the updated version will appear at this address.
